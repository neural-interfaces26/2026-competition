EMG-to-Pose

Regresses 20 hand-joint angle trajectories from 16-channel wrist surface EMG.

The controlled shift combines held-out users and movement stages, testing whether a model survives changes in anatomy, device placement and hand kinematics.

The task

Your model receives torch batches of 16-channel EMG windows X: (B, C, T) and must return joint-angle sequences, predict(X) -> (B, n_joints, T), in degrees. Predictions on a coarser time axis are nearest-resampled to the target's T. Scoring is the mean absolute angular error, averaged over joints and time.

The seed corpus is emg2pose (Salter2024), 16-channel wrist sEMG with UmeTrack joint angles.

Starting-kit note. This track's starting kit currently ships only the zero-dependency Simulated dataset — EMG windows are a linear mixture of the joint angles plus noise, so a per-time-step linear readout recovers the pose above the noise floor. It exercises the full submission contract end to end. The real-data loader is being finalised with the dataset providers and lands with the warm-up data release; the submission contract above will not change.
