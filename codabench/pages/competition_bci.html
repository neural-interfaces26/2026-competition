Neural Interfaces 2026 · Track 02 · Cross-session shift

BCI decoding

Decodes a cued mental command — motor imagery, mental calculation, word association — from short EEG windows.

The controlled shift is cross-session: models train on early sessions and are scored on later sessions of the same subject, with no per-session recalibration allowed.

The task

Epoched classification: one label per window. Your model receives torch batches X: (B, C, T) and must return one predicted class per window, predict(X) -> (B,). Scoring is balanced accuracy over the held-out split, so every class counts equally regardless of its frequency.

The data comes through the neuralbench eeg/motor_imagery task config (4-s stimulus windows, subject-level split). The starting kit exposes three study overlays: stieger2021 (Stieger2021Continuous — the public proxy, 62 subjects, 4-class, large download), tangermann2012 (BNCI2014_001 — small, handy for real-data smoke tests) and dreyer2023 (Dreyer2023Large — official-candidate dataset, 2-class, predefined train/test subjects). A zero-dependency Simulated dataset ships for no-network smoke tests.
