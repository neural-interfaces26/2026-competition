# Timeline

**Warm-up extended:** warm-up and registration remain open through Sunday, November 1 at 23:59. The sealed final runs Thursday, November 5 at 00:01 to Saturday, November 21 at 23:59.

**All November deadlines below use CET (UTC+01:00, Europe/Paris).**

## Warm-up phase

**Sep 21 through Sunday, Nov 1, 2026 at 23:59 CET (UTC+01:00)**

Select **Warm-up Phase (update 1)** for new submissions; earlier warm-up phases remain legacy.

Codabench evaluates submissions on public, track-specific test data during warm-up. Because these data and labels are public, overlap or leakage is possible. Use the indicative leaderboard to validate the submission workflow and iterate on your model. Warm-up scores do not determine the final ranking.

**Submission limit:** 5 submissions per participant per day per track, with **100 submissions total per participant per track** for the warm-up phase.

## Phase transition — no new submissions

**Monday, Nov 2 at 00:01 through Wednesday, Nov 4 at 23:59 CET (UTC+01:00)**

No new warm-up or sealed submissions are accepted between the warm-up closing and the sealed opening.

## Sealed final phase

**Thursday, Nov 5 at 00:01 through Saturday, Nov 21, 2026 at 23:59 CET (UTC+01:00)**

This provides **17 calendar days** of sealed evaluation.

Codabench switches to the private 2026 evaluation cohorts. Their examples and labels remain hidden. Codabench automatically displays your or your team's **best eligible sealed score**, which is retained for the final ranking subject to the reproducibility audit. Solo participants count as one-person teams. Warm-up scores do not count toward the final ranking.

**Submission limit:** 1 submission per participant per day per track, with **5 submissions total per participant per track** for the entire sealed phase.

Status and aggregate leaderboard scores remain visible; execution logs, prediction files and scoring-step output are hidden. See the [Submission Guide](participate.md) for local validation and the [Participation rules](terms.md) for the full policy.

## Reproducibility audit

**Nov 22–30, 2026**

The top three teams in each track undergo a reproducibility audit. Organizers rerun the submitted training pipeline and evaluate the resulting model weights on the sealed test set.

**Winner notifications are targeted for December 1**, after audit validation, ahead of the workshop. Finalists should have their training and inference code, configuration, weights and data disclosures ready when the sealed phase closes.

## Winners’ ceremony

**Dec 11 and 12, 2026**

The winners will be announced during the Brain and Body Workshop at NeurIPS 2026 in Sydney.
