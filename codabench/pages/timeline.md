# Timeline · submissions closed

**Well done to everyone who participated in the EEG/EMG Foundation Challenge 2026!** Registration closed for all four tracks, and sealed submissions closed **Saturday, November 21 at 23:59 CET (UTC+01:00, Europe/Paris)**.

## Final evaluations and results

An upload completed with a submission ID before the deadline remains eligible for evaluation. Evaluation can start and finish after closure. **Please do not cancel or delete queued or running submissions.** Organizers continue monitoring and recovering platform incidents.

Aggregate scores and statuses remain visible. Execution logs, prediction files and scoring-step outputs remain hidden. Rankings remain provisional until the reproducibility audit and eligibility checks are complete.

## Reproducibility audit

**Nov 22–30, 2026**

The top three teams in each track undergo a reproducibility audit. Organizers rerun the submitted training pipeline and evaluate the resulting model weights on the sealed test set, as described in the [Participation rules](terms.md).

Finalists will be contacted with instructions. Keep the exact submitted weights, training and inference code, configurations, environment details and data/pretraining disclosures ready. **Winner notifications are targeted for December 1**, after audit validation.

## Winners’ ceremony

**Dec 11 and 12, 2026**

Winners will be celebrated during the [Brain and Body Workshop](https://brainbodyfm-workshop.github.io) at NeurIPS 2026 in Sydney. Thank you to all participants, track teams and contributors for this collective effort.

## Completed phases · historical reference

- **Warm-up** ran September 21 to November 1, 2026. Its public-data scores are archived and do not count toward the final ranking.
- **Transition** ran November 2–4, with no new submissions.
- **Sealed evaluation** began for Tracks 01, 02 and 04 on Thursday, November 5 at 00:01 CET (UTC+01:00), giving those tracks 17 calendar days. All four tracks closed on Saturday, November 21 at 23:59 CET (UTC+01:00).
- Historical total allowances were **5 submissions total per team per track**, **7 in Track 02** and **12 in Track 03**. Daily limits were raised to match these totals for the final days. No new submissions are accepted.

Only the team’s best eligible sealed score across its members is retained for the final ranking, subject to the audit. Solo participants count as one-person teams. Public training data, Scientific FAQs and [local replay workflows](participate.md) remain available for research and reproducibility.
